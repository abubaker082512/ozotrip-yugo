01. New Logo Concepts

Concept 1 (Recommended)

The "Infinite Flight & Compass"

An aerodynamic infinity 'O' loop crossed by a soaring supersonic jet with warm sunset contrails and a subtle north compass star. Communicates worldwide connectivity, speed, confidence, and wanderlust.

  • Dynamic Flight Jet
  • Horizon Sunset Arc
  • North Star Navigation
  • Perfect at Any Scale
Concept 2

The "Compass Peak & Waypoint"

Combines a modern map waypoint pin with snow-capped mountain peaks (celebrating Northern Pakistan, Hunza, Passu Cones) and a golden navigational star. Ideal for adventure, nature tours, and sacred pilgrimages.

  • Geo-Location Pin
  • Himalayan Mountain Peaks
  • Golden Guide Star
  • Adventure & Heritage
Concept 3

The "Horizon Orbit & Modern Wings"

An ultra-sleek, minimalist dual-ribbon monogram combining an orbital loop with stylized flight wings in vibrant Indigo and Sunset Coral. Gives OzoTrips a high-tech Silicon-Valley booking portal vibe.

  • Modernist Geometric Monogram
  • Tech & Seamless UI Friendly
  • Dual Ribbon Orbit
  • Coral & Indigo Pulse
