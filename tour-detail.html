Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

1

Day 1: Arrival in Skardu & Shangrila Lake Check-in

Land at Skardu International Airport with scenic views of K2 ranges. Private Prado transfer to Lower Kachura Lake (Shangrila Resort). Evening leisure walk along the lake and Upper Kachura trout stream.

🚗 Drive: 45 Mins ⛰️ Altitude: 2,230m 🍳 Meals: Dinner
2

Day 2: Katpana Cold Desert & Shigar Fort Palace

Morning excursion to the world’s highest cold desert at Katpana. Enjoy quad biking across snow-capped sand dunes. Continue towards the historical 400-year-old Serena Shigar Fort palace for traditional Balti lunch.

🚗 Drive: 2.5 Hours ⛰️ Altitude: 2,260m 🍳 Meals: Breakfast, Lunch
3

Day 3: Manthoka Waterfall & Khaplu Royal Palace

Journey into Ghanche district to witness the 180-foot Manthoka Waterfall. Tour the Raja Palace of Khaplu and 700-year-old Chaqchan Mosque. Overnight stay at Serena Khaplu Palace.

🚗 Drive: 3.5 Hours ⛰️ Altitude: 2,600m 🍳 Meals: Breakfast
4

Day 4: Scenic Drive via Karakoram Highway to Hunza Valley

Spectacular highway drive along Indus & Gilgit river confluence. Stop at Rakaposhi Viewpoint (7,788m) for fresh mountain tea. Check-in to luxury panoramic resort in Karimabad, Central Hunza.

🚗 Drive: 5 Hours ⛰️ Altitude: 2,438m 🍳 Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
5

Day 5: Baltit Fort, Altit Fort & Eagle's Nest Sunset

Guided tour of ancient UNESCO-restored Baltit and Altit Forts. Sample local Hunza apricot walnut cakes in Karimabad bazaar. Drive up to Eagle's Nest viewpoint at Duikar for unforgettable sunset over 6 peaks above 7,000m.

🚗 Drive: 1 Hour ⛰️ Altitude: 2,850m 🍳 Meals: Breakfast
6

Day 6: Attabad Lake Speedboat & Passu Cones Cathedral

Private speedboat cruise over turquoise Attabad Lake. Walk the thrilling Hussaini Suspension Bridge and gaze up at the razor-sharp Cathedral of Passu Cones. Return to Karimabad for musical cultural night.

🚗 Drive: 3 Hours ⛰️ Altitude: 2,500m 🍳 Meals: Breakfast, BBQ Dinner
